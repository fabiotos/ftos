1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fabio Silva ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile applications and games (the "Apps").

We aim to keep the Apps' privacy practices transparent. This policy primarily describes the information processed in connection with advertising provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

2. Information processed in connection with advertising

Our Apps use Google's mobile advertising services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), to display advertisements in the Apps. When these services are used, Google and its advertising partners may process information associated with the device and the Apps in order to provide, measure, personalize, and protect advertising services, subject to the user's choices and applicable law.

Depending on the advertising configuration and the user's choices, information processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as an advertising identifier or other device/app identifiers.
  • Advertising information, such as advertisements delivered or interacted with.
  • Game/app interaction information, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, hang/crash-related information, or other technical information used to operate and improve advertising services.

Google states that the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share certain information automatically for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. The exact information and purposes can depend on the SDK version, advertising configuration, and optional features that are enabled.

3. How advertising information is used

Advertising-related information may be used to provide and deliver ads, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve advertising services. Where applicable, information may also be used for personalized advertising based on the user's choices and applicable law.

We use Google's consent-management mechanisms where required to present users with privacy choices. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, applicable consent choices are presented through Google's consent-management flow.

4. Your privacy choices

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

Where required, users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are presented with Google's consent message and can make choices about the use of information for advertising purposes. Users can change or withdraw applicable consent through the privacy controls made available in the Apps.

United States

Where applicable, users in U.S. states with relevant privacy requirements are presented with privacy choices through Google's consent-management system. These choices may include options concerning the sale or sharing of personal information and other rights provided by applicable state law.

If you have a privacy-choice or consent-management control available in the Apps, use that control to review or change your applicable choices.

our Apps uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide advertising. Google's processing of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data retention and security

We do not independently control the retention periods applied by Google to information processed through Google's advertising services. Google determines retention periods for information it processes according to its own policies and applicable requirements.

Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is protected in transit using industry-standard transport security mechanisms.

7. Children's privacy

our Apps is not intended to request personal information directly from users. We do not knowingly ask users to provide information such as their name, postal address, or email address through the Apps unless a future feature expressly requires it and this Privacy Policy is updated accordingly.

Advertising and audience settings may be subject to additional requirements depending on the age of users and the applicable law. We will configure the Apps and its advertising services according to the requirements applicable to the audience for which the Apps is distributed.
